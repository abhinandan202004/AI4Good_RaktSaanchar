# Blood Donation FAQ

## What is blood donation?
Blood donation is the voluntary process of giving blood to help patients in need.

## How often can a person donate blood?
Whole blood can generally be donated every 3 months for men and every 4 months for women, depending on local regulations.

## Is blood donation safe?
Yes. Sterile, single-use equipment is used for every donor.

## How long does blood donation take?
The entire process usually takes 30–45 minutes.

## What should I do before donating?
- Stay hydrated
- Eat a healthy meal
- Get adequate sleep

## What should I do after donating?
- Drink fluids
- Avoid heavy exercise for 24 hours
- Rest if feeling dizzy
