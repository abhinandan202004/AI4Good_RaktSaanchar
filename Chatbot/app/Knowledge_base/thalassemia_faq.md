# Thalassemia FAQ

## What is thalassemia?
Thalassemia is an inherited blood disorder that affects hemoglobin production.

## Why do thalassemia patients need blood transfusions?
Regular transfusions help maintain healthy hemoglobin levels.

## Is thalassemia curable?
Bone marrow transplantation may offer a cure in some cases.

## What complications can occur?
- Iron overload
- Heart complications
- Growth delays

## Why are blood donors important?
Regular blood donors help ensure a stable blood supply for transfusion-dependent patients.
