# Donor Eligibility Rules

## Minimum Age
18 years

## Maximum Age
65 years

## Minimum Weight
50 kg

## Basic Eligibility
- Healthy individual
- No active infection
- Meets age requirements
- Meets weight requirements

## Temporary Deferrals
- Recent surgery
- Recent illness
- Certain medications

## Permanent Deferrals
- Certain chronic diseases
- High-risk medical conditions
